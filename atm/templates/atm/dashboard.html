Help / FAQ

How do I change my PIN?

Open the menu, choose Change PIN, enter your current PIN, then your new 4-digit PIN twice.

What are the withdrawal limits?

Checking allows ₱25,000 per transaction and Savings ₱10,000. Each account has a daily limit of ₱50,000. See Withdrawal limits in the menu for what you have left today.

Why was my withdrawal declined?

Your balance may be too low, you may have passed a transaction or daily limit, or the withdrawal would leave less than the ₱500 minimum balance.

What happens if I enter the wrong PIN?

After 4 wrong attempts the PIN is locked. You can reset the lock from the PIN screen.

How do I log out?

Choose Logout in the menu. Always log out when you walk away from the ATM.

Will the bank ask for my PIN?

Never. Do not share your PIN with anyone, including bank staff.

What amounts can I deposit or withdraw?

Whole pesos in multiples of ₱50, with no coins or cents. The minimum deposit is ₱500 and the minimum withdrawal is ₱100.
